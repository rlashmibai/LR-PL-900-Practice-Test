My goal here is simple: help you pass PL-900 on your first attempt. Come to it with a learning-and-evaluating mindset, and you'll walk into exam day with no second-guessing and no surprises.

Sound familiar? These are the exact gaps this practice test is built to close:

  1. Not being sure when Dataverse is the right call instead of SharePoint or Excel
  2. Not knowing what connectors actually are, or how standard connectors differ from premium ones
  3. Being unclear on the difference between automated, instant, scheduled, and business process flows
  4. Not understanding how Copilot features show up across Power Apps, Power Automate, and Power Pages

  6. Not knowing how solutions are used to package and move apps and flows between environments
  7. Being fuzzy on how topics, entities, and actions fit together
  8. Not knowing how to connect a copilot to a Power Automate flow
  9. Not seeing how environments, solutions, and security roles all connect
  10. Being unsure which licenses are actually required for Power Apps, Power Automate, or Copilot Studio
