💡 Why This Practice Test Is Worth Your Time

My goal here is simple: help you pass PL-900 on your first attempt. Come to it with a learning-and-evaluating mindset, and you'll walk into exam day with no second-guessing and no surprises.

Sound familiar? These are the exact gaps this practice test is built to close:

  1. Not being sure when Dataverse is the right call instead of SharePoint or Excel
  2. Not knowing what connectors actually are, or how standard connectors differ from premium ones
  3. Being unclear on the difference between automated, instant, scheduled, and business process flows
  4. Not understanding how Copilot features show up across Power Apps, Power Automate, and Power Pages
  5. Confusing business rules with Power Automate flows, or not knowing how they differ
  6. Not knowing how solutions are used to package and move apps and flows between environments
  7. Being fuzzy on how topics, entities, and actions fit together
  8. Not knowing how to connect a copilot to a Power Automate flow
  9. Not seeing how environments, solutions, and security roles all connect
  10. Being unsure which licenses are actually required for Power Apps, Power Automate, or Copilot Studio

By the time you finish working through this practice test, you'll genuinely own your knowledge, from the smallest feature to the most advanced concept.

Working through focused, topic-by-topic questions sharpens your skills and gets you into the exam room with clarity, confidence, and no gaps left in your preparation.

The goal is simple: to have you thinking, "I've practiced 600 questions, I know the concepts, I'm confident in my answers, and I won't waste time second-guessing myself."

Privacy Policy

What this site collects

You can take every practice test on this site with no sign-in at all. If you choose to sign in to save your score history, all that's asked for is a name and email address, no password. That name/email and your score history are stored only in your own browser's local storage (localStorage). They are never transmitted to, or stored on, any server or database run by this site, because this site doesn't have one, it's a static page.

That also means your data lives only on the device and browser you signed in with. Clearing your browser data, or switching devices/browsers, will clear it too, and there's no way for me to recover it for you.

Contact form

If you use the Contact page, the name, email, and message you submit are sent via FormSubmit.co, a third-party form-delivery service, to reach the site owner's inbox. That information is used only to respond to you, never for marketing, and never sold or shared with anyone else.

No ads, no tracking

This site runs no advertising and no third-party analytics or advertising-tracking scripts. The only external resources loaded are Google Fonts (for the site's typeface) and, each time you start a practice test, a single anonymous ping to a private Google Sheet the site owner uses to see roughly how many tests are being started. That ping contains only a timestamp and which test was opened, no name, email, or other personal data, and it is never tied to you individually.

Cookie Policy

This site does not use cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics.

To remember an optional sign-in (name and email) and your score history between visits, the site uses your browser's local storage (localStorage) rather than cookies. That data stays on your device and is never sent to a server.

The site's font is loaded from Google Fonts. Loading a font from Google's CDN can involve a minimal technical request to Google's servers; Google's own privacy policy covers how that request is handled on their end.
